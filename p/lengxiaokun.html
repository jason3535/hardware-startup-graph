1992年生，13岁起接触机器人，就读于哈尔滨工业大学，曾获国内外人形机器人冠军。2016年带领哈工大团队创办乐聚（深圳）机器人，早期获松禾资本1000万元天使投资。2023年12月发布高动态全尺寸人形机器人KUAVO（夸父），超90%零部件国产化。截至2025年已交付第100台全尺寸人形机器人，并推动人形机器人开源生态。
Born 1992,Leng began robotics at 13 and studied at Harbin Institute of Technology,winning national and international humanoid-robot championships. In 2016 he founded Leju (Shenzhen) Robotics,leading an HIT team,with an early 10M yuan angel round from Songhe Capital. In December 2023 the team unveiled the high-dynamic KUAVO (Kuafu) full-size humanoid; over 90% of its parts are domestically made. By 2025 Leju had delivered its 100th full-size humanoid and pushed an open-source humanoid ecosystem.
