Anduril Industries

Oculus VR & Anduril 创始人 · $610亿防务科技

ar-vroverseas
1992年生于长滩。19岁创办Oculus VR，2012年Kickstarter筹$240万，2014年$20亿卖给Facebook，2017年被解雇。随即与前Palantir高管联创Anduril——建造自主无人机和AI军事硬件。2026 年中再融资约 50 亿美元、估值翻倍至约 610 亿（Thrive 与 a16z 领投），并拿下美国陆军 10 年、最高 200 亿美元的企业级合同（2026.3）。俄亥俄州 500 万平方尺 Arsenal-1 工厂在建，预计 2026-27 IPO。2025 年底被中国制裁。
Born 1992 in Long Beach, CA. Founded Oculus VR at age 19, Kickstarter raised $2.4M in 2012, sold to Facebook for $2B in 2014, fired in 2017. Immediately co-founded Anduril Industries with ex-Palantir executives (Trae Stephens, Brian Schimpf) — building autonomous drones, AI-powered military hardware, and sensor towers. In mid-2026 Anduril raised ~$5B to roughly double its valuation to ~$61B (led by Thrive Capital and a16z), and won a 10-year US Army enterprise contract worth up to $20B (Mar 2026). Building the 5M sq ft Arsenal-1 factory in Ohio ahead of a likely 2026-27 IPO. Sanctioned by China Dec 2025 for Taiwan arms sales.
