Amazon (Devices & Services)

亚马逊设备负责人 · 前微软 Surface 掌门

consumersmart-home
在微软主导 Surface 产品线近 20 年（首席产品官），2023 年加盟亚马逊执掌设备与服务。主导 Alexa+（Alexa 的生成式 AI 重构），并将亚马逊硬件重新分层为 entry/core/signature（高端）三档，预告未来 Alexa+ 可穿戴与 AR 眼镜；还被曝主导一个由 Alexa+ 驱动、代号「Transformer」的 AI 优先手机项目。是亚马逊在消费级 AI 硬件上叫板苹果的核心人物。
Built and led Microsoft's Surface line for ~20 years (Chief Product Officer) before joining Amazon in 2023 to run its Devices & Services group. Oversaw Alexa+, the generative-AI relaunch of Alexa, and is re-tiering Amazon's hardware into entry / core / signature (premium) lines, teasing future Alexa+ wearables and AR glasses. Also reportedly behind an Alexa+-powered, AI-first mobile project codenamed 'Transformer.' A central figure in Amazon's bid to challenge Apple in consumer AI hardware.
