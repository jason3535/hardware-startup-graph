清华本硕、南加大（USC）机器人学习博士，是较早把注意力机制引入神经网络体系的研究者之一。2023 年 12 月创办自变量机器人（X Square Robot），自研『通用具身智能大模型』。成立两年多完成 9 轮融资、累计超 30 亿元，含 2026 年 4 月约 20 亿元 B 轮（小米战投、红杉中国领投）——是国内唯一同时获字节、美团、阿里、小米四家大厂投资的具身智能公司，估值跃居赛道首位。
Tsinghua BS/MS and USC robotics-learning PhD; an early explorer of attention mechanisms in neural networks. Founded X Square Robot (自变量机器人) in Dec 2023 to build a general embodied-intelligence foundation model. Across 9 rounds in ~2 years it raised RMB 3B+, including a ~RMB 2B Series B (Apr 2026, led by Xiaomi and Sequoia China) — the only embodied-AI startup backed by all four of ByteDance, Meituan, Alibaba and Xiaomi, with a sector-leading valuation.
