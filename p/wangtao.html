1980年生于杭州。港科大就读，教授李泽湘发现其才华。宿舍打造原型机，2006年移师深圳。2013年发布 Phantom，引爆全球无人机革命。DJI 占全球约 70-80% 无人机市场。2025 年 12 月 FCC 将大疆列入「covered list」，实质封锁其新品在美销售（大疆警告 2026 年约 15 亿美元美国营收承压，涉 14 款在售 + 25 款新品）；数周后北京自 2026 年 5 月 1 日起禁售消费级无人机——全球最大无人机厂商两头受挤。身家约 50 亿美元。
Born 1980 in Hangzhou. Attended HKUST where professor Li Zexiang noticed his talent. Built DJI prototypes in his dorm room. Released Phantom in 2013, igniting the global drone revolution. DJI dominates ~70-80% of the global drone market. In Dec 2025 the FCC added DJI to its Covered List, effectively blocking new-product sales in the US — DJI warned of ~$1.5B in 2026 US revenue at risk across 14 existing and 25 new products; weeks later Beijing banned consumer drone sales from May 1 2026, squeezing the world's largest drone maker from both sides. Net worth ~$5B.
