来杰 Lai Jie

Stardust Intelligence (Astribot)

星尘智能创始人兼CEO · 腾讯Robotics X首位员工

robotics
毕业于西安电子科技大学，五邑大学智能系统硕士。约16年机器人研发经验。曾在香港理工大学任助理研究员，后在百度负责小度机器人项目。2018年成为腾讯Robotics X首位员工，主导Ollie轮腿机器人研发。2022年离开腾讯，在深圳联合创办星尘智能（Astribot）。绳驱双臂机器人S1于2024年4月走红，T系列于2026年5月发布、起售价8.99万元。2025年完成A/A+/A++数亿元融资（蚂蚁集团、锦秋基金及国科系资本参投）；2026年6月披露B轮融资超10亿元——3个月内连融3轮——投后估值突破百亿元，自称全球首个量产绳驱AI机器人公司（2025年底启动千台级交付）。创始核心团队6人均出自腾讯Robotics X。
Studied at Xidian University,master's in intelligent systems at Wuyi University. ~16 years in robotics. Assistant researcher at HK PolyU,then led Baidu's Xiaodu robot project. Became the first employee of Tencent Robotics X in 2018,leading the Ollie wheeled-legged robot. Left in 2022 to co-found Stardust Intelligence (Astribot) in Shenzhen. The cable-driven Astribot S1 dual-arm robot went viral April 2024; the T-series launched May 2026 from RMB 89,900. In June 2026 Astribot disclosed a Series B of over RMB 1 billion — its third round in three months — pushing post-money valuation past RMB 10 billion, and claims to be the world's first to mass-produce cable-driven AI robots (1,000-unit-scale delivery began end-2025). Its six-person founding core all came out of Tencent Robotics X.
