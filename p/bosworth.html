安德鲁·博斯沃思（Boz） Andrew Bosworth

Meta (Reality Labs)

Meta CTO · Reality Labs 与 AI 眼镜

ar-vrconsumer
Meta 首席技术官、Reality Labs 负责人（Quest 头显与 Ray-Ban Meta 眼镜所在部门），Facebook 早期工程师、News Feed 缔造者。2026 年 Reality Labs 累计亏损约 700 亿美元后，Meta 在该部门裁员约 1500 人，把资源从元宇宙转向 AI 智能眼镜——其增长最快的赌注，Ray-Ban Meta 已售出 200 万+ 副，计划 2026 年底将眼镜产能翻倍。博斯沃思（Boz）于 2026.5.24 罕见地现场演示 AR 眼镜原型，并主张 AI 而非 VR 才是未来可穿戴的关键入口。
Meta's CTO and head of Reality Labs, the division behind Quest headsets and Ray-Ban Meta glasses; an early Facebook engineer who built News Feed. In 2026, after Reality Labs accumulated ~$70B in cumulative losses, Meta cut ~1,500 staff there and shifted resources from the metaverse toward AI smart glasses — its fastest-growing bet, with 2M+ Ray-Ban Meta units sold and plans to double glasses production capacity by end-2026. Bosworth ('Boz') gave a rare live AR-glasses prototype demo (May 24 2026) and argues AI, not VR, will be the key interface for future wearables.
