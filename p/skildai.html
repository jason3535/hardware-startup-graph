Skild AI 联合创始人兼 CEO · 机器人基础模型

roboticsoverseas
前卡内基梅隆大学教授，2023 年与同为 CMU 教授的 Abhinav Gupta 共同创办 Skild AI。Skild 主打一个『全形态（omni-bodied）』基础模型 Skild Brain——无需为每个机器人单独重训，即可控制任意机器人完成任意任务，并能实时适应身体或环境的剧烈变化。2026 年 1 月完成 14 亿美元 C 轮、估值超 140 亿美元（软银领投，英伟达、贝索斯、三星、LG、施耐德、Salesforce Ventures 跟投），七个月内估值从 45 亿翻三倍，累计融资超 20 亿美元。
Former Carnegie Mellon professor who co-founded Skild AI in 2023 with fellow CMU professor Abhinav Gupta. Skild builds a single 'omni-bodied' foundation model — the Skild Brain — meant to control any robot for any task without bespoke retraining, adapting in real time to changes in body or environment. In Jan 2026 Skild raised a $1.4B Series C at a $14B+ valuation (led by SoftBank, with Nvidia, Bezos Expeditions, Samsung, LG, Schneider, Salesforce Ventures), tripling its $4.5B mark in seven months; $2B+ raised to date.
