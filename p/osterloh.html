Google 高级副总裁，2024 年重组后统管合并的「平台与设备」部门（Pixel、Android、Chrome、Photos），此前任摩托罗拉总裁。2026 I/O 大会与三星及眼镜伙伴 Warby Parker、Gentle Monster 共同发布 Android XR 智能眼镜——由 Gemini 驱动的音频版与显示版眼镜，2026 年 10 月 14 日开售（基础款 $799；11 月推出无屏音频款 $299；Gentle Monster 高端款 2027 初）。这是 Google 迄今对 Meta Ray-Ban 眼镜最直接的回应，Gemini 作为导航、翻译、搜索的主入口。
Senior VP leading Google's merged Platforms & Devices group (Pixel, Android, Chrome, Photos) since a 2024 reorg that put hardware and Android under one roof; previously president of Motorola. At I/O 2026 he helped unveil Android XR smart glasses built with Samsung and eyewear partners Warby Parker and Gentle Monster — Gemini-powered audio and display eyewear shipping Oct 14 2026 ($799 base; a screenless audio variant at $299 in November; a Gentle Monster premium model in early 2027). It is Google's most direct answer yet to Meta's Ray-Ban glasses, with Gemini as the primary interface for navigation, translation and search.
