科技连续创业者（先前创办 Sundae）。2019 年与 Josh Clemente（前 SpaceX）和 Casey Means 医生联合创办 Levels。软件层架在 Dexcom CGM 上 — 把食物对血糖的反应可视化，优化代谢健康。2022 年 5 月 A 轮 3800 万美金，估值 3 亿。乘上代谢健康赛道，Casey Means 后来因《Good Energy》一书被广泛知晓。
Tech entrepreneur (founded Sundae prev). Co-founded Levels Health 2019 with Josh Clemente (ex-SpaceX) and Dr. Casey Means. Software platform on top of Dexcom CGM — visualizes blood glucose response to foods, optimizes metabolic health. $38M Series A May 2022, $300M valuation. Riding the metabolic health wave; Casey Means later went mainstream via 'Good Energy' book.
