robotics
2010年与三位朋友以20万元在上海创办擎朗智能。2016年推出DINERBOT T1，号称全球首款自主配送机器人。据IDC，2024年擎朗在全球配送及餐饮机器人市场排名第一，占全球出货量22.7%，累计售出超10万台商用机器人，覆盖配送、清洁、迎宾等场景。2024年推出首款双臂具身服务机器人，后发布面向酒旅的XMAN-F1人形机器人。
Founded Keenon Robotics in Shanghai in 2010 with three friends and 200,000 yuan. Launched the DINERBOT T1 in 2016,billed as the world's first autonomous delivery robot. Keenon topped IDC's 2024 global rankings for delivery and food-service robots with 22.7% of worldwide shipments,having sold 100,000+ commercial robots across delivery,cleaning and reception. In 2024 it launched its first dual-arm embodied service robot and later the XMAN-F1 humanoid for hospitality.
