裴宇 Carl Pei

Nothing

Nothing 创始人 · 一加联合创始人

consumer
1989生于北京，瑞典长大。斯德哥尔摩经济学院辍学。诺基亚实习→魅族→OPPO 2012。与刘作虎联合创办一加（2013.12）。2020.10离开。2021.1在伦敦创办Nothing。透明Glyph设计。2025.9 完成 2 亿美元 C 轮、估值 13 亿美元（Tiger Global 领投，GV/EQT/高通创投/Nikhil Kamath 等跟投），用于打造 AI 原生设备与 Nothing 自研「超个性化、主动式」OS。2026.3 裴宇判断 AI 智能体将取代传统手机 App；Nothing 首款 AI 原生设备（明确不是手机）预计 2026 年问世。
Born 1989 Beijing, grew up Sweden. Stockholm Sch of Econ dropout. Nokia intern, Meizu, OPPO 2012. Co-founded OnePlus Dec 2013 with Pete Lau. Left Oct 2020. Nothing London Jan 2021. Transparent Glyph Interface design. Raised a $200M Series C at a $1.3B valuation (Sep 2025, led by Tiger Global with GV, EQT, Qualcomm Ventures, Tapestry, Highland Europe and Nikhil Kamath) to build AI-native devices and Nothing's own 'hyper-customized,' proactive OS. In Mar 2026 Pei argued AI agents will make traditional smartphone apps obsolete; Nothing's first AI-native device — explicitly not a phone — is slated for 2026.
