打造 Apple Vision Pro 头显的苹果高管。2025 年 4 月，在苹果「更个性化 Siri」跳票成为公开尴尬后，苹果让罗克韦尔接管 Siri（从 John Giannandrea 手中），他用 Vision Pro 团队重组了 Siri 团队。正以 LLM 架构重写 Siri，预计随 iOS 27 推出；苹果一再推迟的智能家居中枢据报道也卡在这版个性化 Siri 上。罗克韦尔仍负责 visionOS，并据报道考虑在 Siri 改造完成后退居顾问角色。
The Apple executive who created the Vision Pro headset. In April 2025, after Apple's delayed 'more personal Siri' became a public embarrassment, Apple put Rockwell in charge of Siri (taking it from John Giannandrea) and he restructured the team with his Vision Pro engineers. He is re-architecting Siri around an LLM-based system, now expected to ship with iOS 27; Apple's long-delayed smart-home hub is reportedly gated on that personalized Siri being ready. Rockwell still leads visionOS, and has reportedly weighed stepping back to an advisory role once the Siri overhaul lands.
