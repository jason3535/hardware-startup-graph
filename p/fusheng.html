傅盛是猎豹移动董事长兼CEO，2016年创办北京猎户星空，打造AI服务机器人与语音交互操作系统。猎户星空的机器人销往60多个国家；2024年一季度海外机器人销量已超过国内。2024年其大模型算法通过备案，2025年2月接入DeepSeek升级语音交互能力。傅盛公开表示机器人行业短期不会爆发式增长，而是持续稳定、逐步加速的过程。
Chairman and CEO of Cheetah Mobile,Fu Sheng founded OrionStar (Beijing) in 2016 to build AI service robots and a voice-interaction OS. OrionStar's robots are marketed in 60+ countries; in Q1 2024 its overseas robot sales surpassed domestic. In 2024 its large-model algorithm passed Chinese regulatory filing,and in February 2025 OrionStar integrated DeepSeek to upgrade voice interaction. Fu has publicly argued the robot industry will see steady,gradual acceleration rather than near-term explosive growth.
