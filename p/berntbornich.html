挪威机器人学家。2014年在挪威莫斯创办1X Technologies（原Halodi Robotics）。NEO家用机器人（身高约168cm、仅30kg，主打比大型对手更安全）开放预售：2万美元 Early Access（200美元定金、2026优先交付）或 499美元/月订阅。2026 年先在美国交付、2027 起拓展其他市场；首发能力含开门、取物、关灯，随软件更新升级。OpenAI 为战略投资人。双总部：帕洛阿尔托+挪威莫斯，加州 Hayward 制造。
Norwegian roboticist. Founded 1X Technologies (originally Halodi Robotics) in 2014 in Moss, Norway. Building general-purpose humanoid robots. The NEO Home Robot (about 168cm tall, a lightweight 30kg frame meant to be safer than larger rivals) opened pre-orders at $20,000 Early Access ($200 deposit, priority 2026 delivery) or a $499/month subscription. Deliveries start in the US in 2026, expanding to other markets from 2027; Day-1 tasks include opening doors, fetching items and turning off lights, improving via software updates. OpenAI is a strategic investor. Dual HQ: Palo Alto + Moss, Norway. Manufacturing in Hayward, CA.
