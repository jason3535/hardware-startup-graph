2024 年 1 月从地平线机器人事业部拆分独立（深圳南山），地瓜机器人定位具身智能的『卖水人』——做覆盖芯片+算法+软件+开发工具的机器人通用底座，而非自研本体。CEO 王丛（北京工业大学计算机本科、美国莱特州立大学博士；曾任职阿里、联创文因互联）拆分前在地平线负责 AIoT 与机器人事业部。RDK（机器人开发套件）系列从 RDK X3/X5（10 TOPS）到 RDK S100（100 TOPS 单 SoC 算控一体），并计划 2026 年推出 500+ TOPS、承载端到端『AGI 型机器人』的高算力 SoC；核心 BPU 由地平线授权。已完成 A 轮 1 亿美元（2025.5）、B1 1.2 亿 + B2 1.5 亿美元（2026.3–4，滴滴、美团龙珠等参投），累计披露超 3.7 亿美元。
Spun out of Horizon Robotics' robot division as an independent company in Jan 2024 (Shenzhen), D-Robotics is a 'picks-and-shovels' platform for embodied AI — a universal robot base spanning chips, algorithms, software and dev tools rather than building its own robot bodies. CEO Wang Cong (Beijing Univ of Technology CS, Wright State PhD; ex-Alibaba, co-founder of Memect) led Horizon's AIoT and robotics lines before the spin-off. Its RDK (Robot Developer Kit) line runs from RDK X3/X5 (10 TOPS) to the RDK S100 (100 TOPS, a single-SoC compute-and-control kit), with a 500+ TOPS SoC for end-to-end 'AGI robots' planned for 2026; the core BPU is licensed from Horizon. Raised a $100M Series A (May 2025), then B1 $120M + B2 $150M (Mar–Apr 2026, backers incl. Didi and Meituan's Longzhu), $370M+ disclosed to date.
