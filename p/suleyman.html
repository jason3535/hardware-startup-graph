DeepMind（2010）与 Inflection AI（2022）联合创始人；2024 年 3 月起任微软 AI CEO，主管 Copilot 与微软消费级 AI。2026 Build 大会发布完全自研的 MAI 七模型家族（文本/图像/语音/转写/代码），主张「长期自给自足」，并推出 Copilot 超级 App 与 Scout 智能体。微软硬件布局覆盖 Copilot+ PC，并预告「办公徽章」「桌面伴侣」等环境设备，同时与英伟达大规模合作、在 Windows 端与云上运行 AI 智能体。
Co-founded DeepMind (2010) and Inflection AI (2022); since March 2024 the CEO of Microsoft AI, leading Copilot and Microsoft's consumer AI. At Build 2026 he unveiled the in-house MAI family of seven multimodal models (text, image, voice, transcription and coding) for 'long-term self-sufficiency,' plus a Copilot 'super app' and the Scout agent. Microsoft's hardware push spans Copilot+ PCs and teased ambient devices — 'office badges' and 'desk companions' — alongside a sweeping NVIDIA partnership to run AI agents across Windows devices and the cloud.
