清华大学本硕。团队核心来自清华机器人控制实验室及火神机器人项目，集体具备 20 年+ 人形机器人积累。2023 年 6 月北京创办加速进化科技（Booster Robotics）。理念：为开发者造的机器人，而非展示用机器人。Booster T1：面向全球高校/科研机构的平价全尺寸双足人形。Booster K1：紧凑型室内人形。2026 年 4 月全球累计发货超 700 台，行业出货量前列。2026 年 4 月完成近 10 亿元融资，北京高精尖产业基金 + 京国盛基金 + 华控基金领投，联想之星 / 深创投 / IDG / 源码 / 英诺 / 北京市人工智能产业投资基金等跟投。
Tsinghua University BS+MS. Core team from Tsinghua robotics control lab and the Huoshen Mars Soccer Robot project, with 20+ years collective humanoid experience. Founded Beijing Booster Robotics (加速进化) Jun 2023. Philosophy: robots built for developers, not demos. Booster T1: affordable full-size bipedal humanoid targeting university labs and research institutes globally. Booster K1: compact indoor service humanoid. 700+ units shipped globally by Apr 2026 — among highest shipped volume in the industry. Apr 2026 closed near-1B yuan round led by Beijing High-Tech Industry Fund + Beijing State-Owned Fund + Huakong Fund, with Lenovo Star, Shenzhen Capital, IDG, Source Code Capital, Yingno Fund, and National AI Fund as co-investors.
