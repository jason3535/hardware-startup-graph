希夫曼少年时因2020年打造广受使用的新冠疫情追踪网站而成名。他创办Friend，推出99美元的常听式AI陪伴挂坠，旨在缓解孤独——它不出声，而是通过短信与用户手机实时交流。他以5000万美元估值融资250万美元，投资人包括Perplexity CEO Aravind Srinivas、Solana创始人与Caffeinated Capital，并花费其中180万美元买下friend.com域名。基础版于2025年初开始发货。
Schiffmann gained fame as a teenager for building a widely used COVID-19 tracking website in 2020. He founded Friend,makers of a $99 always-listening AI companion necklace designed to combat loneliness; it communicates by texting the user's phone rather than speaking. He raised $2.5M at a $50M valuation from investors including Perplexity CEO Aravind Srinivas,Solana's founders and Caffeinated Capital,and famously spent $1.8M of it to buy the friend.com domain. The basic device began shipping in early 2025.
