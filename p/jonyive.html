乔尼·艾维 Jony Ive

OpenAI / io

OpenAI AI 设备设计负责人 · 前苹果首席设计官

consumer
英国设计师，获封爵士。任苹果首席设计官近 30 年，缔造 iMac、iPod、iPhone、iPad、Apple Watch，2019 年离开创办设计公司 LoveFrom。2025 年 OpenAI 以约 64 亿美元收购其 AI 硬件创业公司 io，艾维全面负责 OpenAI 与 io 的创意与设计。他与 Sam Altman 正打造一款无屏、语音优先的 AI 陪伴设备（Altman 称其比手机更「平和」），首期计划经富士康量产 4000-5000 万台（从立讯转单，在美国/越南制造）。首款设备原定 2026 下半年亮相，据报道已推迟至不早于 2027；在研多种形态，含耳机型（代号 Sweetpea）与笔型（代号 Gumdrop），且不再沿用「io」之名。
British designer, knighted. Apple's Chief Design Officer for nearly 30 years — shaped the iMac, iPod, iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch — before leaving in 2019 to found the design firm LoveFrom. In 2025 OpenAI acquired his AI-hardware startup io for ~$6.4B, with Ive taking deep creative and design responsibility across OpenAI and io. He and Sam Altman are building a screenless, voice-first AI companion device — described by Altman as more 'peaceful' than a smartphone — with initial production planned at 40-50M units via Foxconn (shifted from Luxshare, made in the US/Vietnam). The first device was targeted for an H2 2026 reveal but reportedly slipped to no earlier than 2027; multiple form factors are being explored, including an earbud ('Sweetpea') and a pen-style device ('Gumdrop'), and the 'io' name is being dropped.
