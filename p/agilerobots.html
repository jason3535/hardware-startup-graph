陈兆芃 Chen Zhaopeng

Agile Robots

思灵机器人联合创始人兼 CEO · 力控机器人

roboticsoverseas
德国宇航中心机器人研究所（DLR/RMC，LWR 力控机械臂的发源地）博士。2018 年联合创办思灵机器人（Agile Robots），慕尼黑与北京双总部，用力控+视觉引导的机器人连接 AI 与物理世界。以 2.2 亿美元 C 轮（软银愿景基金二期领投，高瓴、红杉中国、阿布扎比 Chimera、富士康跟投）跻身估值超 10 亿美元的独角兽。
PhD from the German Aerospace Center's robotics institute (DLR/RMC), birthplace of the LWR force-controlled arm. Co-founded Agile Robots (思灵机器人, est. 2018) with dual HQs in Munich and Beijing, bridging AI and the physical world through force-controlled, vision-guided robots. Reached unicorn status (>$1B) with a $220M Series C led by SoftBank Vision Fund 2 (with Hillhouse, Sequoia China, Abu Dhabi's Chimera and Foxconn).
