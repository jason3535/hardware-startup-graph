Agility Robotics（俄勒冈 Albany）CEO，旗下双足仓储人形机器人 Digit；此前任 Magic Leap CEO，更早在微软任职 24 年（业务拓展 EVP）。Digit 是首个在商业客户现场部署的人形机器人（GXO，2024.6），已搬运 10 万+ 料箱，GXO 签下行业首个多年期 RaaS 协议。Agility 累计融资 6.41 亿美元+（含 4 亿 C 轮）、估值约 20 亿美元，运营年产能 1 万台的 RoboFab 工厂，计划 2026 年底推出安全认证版 Digit、2027 年推出可举 50 磅的第五代。联合创始人为机器人学家 Jonathan Hurst。
CEO of Agility Robotics (Albany, Oregon), maker of the bipedal warehouse humanoid Digit; previously CEO of Magic Leap and a 24-year Microsoft veteran (EVP of business development). Digit was the first humanoid deployed at a commercial customer (GXO, Jun 2024) and has moved 100,000+ totes; GXO signed an industry-first multi-year Robots-as-a-Service deal. Agility has raised $641M+ (incl. a $400M Series C) at ~$2B, runs the 'RoboFab' plant (10,000 units/yr capacity), and plans a safety-certified Digit by late 2026 and a 50-lb-lift Gen-5 for 2027. Co-founded by roboticist Jonathan Hurst.
