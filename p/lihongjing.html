李红京 Li Hongjing

Autel Robotics

道通智能/Autel创始人 · 大疆最大对手

drones
卡内基梅隆大学毕业。连续创业者：先创办道通科技做汽车诊断/电子，2020科创板上市。2014进入无人机瞄准行业应用。2017年用道通科技17%股权换6亿全投无人机。X-STAR（2016消费级）→KESTREL（2018行业级倾转旋翼）。成为大疆全球最大竞争对手（尤其北美）。2024被列入美国商务部实体清单。2025被列入国防部中国军事企业。计划港股IPO。
Carnegie Mellon University graduate. Serial entrepreneur: first founded Autel Tech (Daotong Keji) for automotive diagnostics/electronics, which listed on STAR Board 2020. Entered drones 2014 targeting industrial applications. In 2017, traded 17% of Autel Tech shares for 600M yuan to fund drone business. X-STAR (2016 consumer), KESTREL (2018 industrial tiltrotor). Autel Robotics became DJI's biggest global competitor, especially in North America. 2024: added to US Commerce Entity List. 2025: added to DoD Chinese Military Company list. Despite US sanctions, continues strong global sales. Planning Hong Kong IPO.
