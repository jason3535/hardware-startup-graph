在三个大洲、三个时代都做过消费硬件：谷歌 Android 产品副总裁，经历 Nexus 系列；2013 年出任小米全球副总裁，这次跳槽本身就宣告了中国手机厂商开始国际化；2017 年起在 Facebook 主管 Oculus VR，做完 Quest 几代。2021 年离开，先做 Detect 新冠检测，后联合创办 /dev/agents（智能体操作系统）与 Dreamer（自主助理）。2026 年 3 月 Meta 收购 Dreamer 并把团队一起收进来，他重回 Meta，在 Alexandr Wang 手下的超级智能实验室做贯穿 Meta 各 App 与可穿戴设备的智能体。他反复讲的一句话是：硬件没有 MVP。
Has shipped consumer hardware on three continents and in three eras: VP of Android product at Google through the Nexus years, then global VP at Xiaomi from 2013, the hire that announced China's phone makers as international competitors, then head of Oculus VR at Facebook from 2017 through the Quest launches. Left in 2021, built the Detect COVID test, then co-founded /dev/agents on an operating system for agents and Dreamer on autonomous assistants. In March 2026 Meta acqui-hired Dreamer and he rejoined under Alexandr Wang in Superintelligence Labs, working on agents that run across Meta's apps and wearables. His standing argument: there is no MVP in hardware.
