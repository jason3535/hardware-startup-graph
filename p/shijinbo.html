1982年生于哈尔滨。父母在航天国企工厂。哈工大本硕连读。港科大电子与计算机工程博士，师从李泽湘——与汪滔同门师妹。2009年随李泽湘考察日本美国机器人工厂后，2011年创办李群自动化。2013年研发出全球首台高性能驱控一体并联机器人。创业初期找师兄汪滔拿大疆项目度过困难期。2022年战略融资估值超200亿元。中国轻量级工业机器人领军者。
Born 1982 Harbin. Parents at aerospace state factory. HIT (Harbin Institute of Technology) BS+MS. HKUST PhD in ECE under Li Zexiang — Frank Wang's junior fellow disciple (tong men shi mei). Founded QKM Technology (Liqun Automation) 2011 after visiting Japan/US robot factories with Li. Built world's first high-performance drive-control integrated parallel robot (2013). Early survival: got projects from senior fellow Frank Wang at DJI. Strategic round 2022: valuation >20B yuan. Pioneer of lightweight industrial robots in China.
