2011 年深圳创办 AfterShokz —— 骨传导耳机（开放式，声音通过颞骨传导）。瞄准跑步 / 游泳 / 骑行人群，不能用入耳式耳机的场景。2021 年品牌从 AfterShokz 改为 Shokz。OpenRun Pro、OpenSwim、OpenComm 商务系列。北美 / 欧洲 DTC + 亚马逊渠道强势，2024 年估算营收 3 亿美金+。开放式耳机品类基本是他们开创的，后被 Sony / Bose 跟进。
Founded AfterShokz 2011 in Shenzhen — bone-conduction headphones (open-ear, sound delivered via temple bone). Targeted runners / swimmers / cyclists who can't use earbuds. Rebranded to Shokz 2021. OpenRun Pro, OpenSwim, OpenComm work-pro line. Major DTC + Amazon presence in NA / EU. Estimated revenue ~$300M+ 2024. Open-ear category effectively their invention; now imitated by Sony / Bose.
