丹·西罗克 Dan Siroker

Limitless

Limitless（前Rewind）创始人兼CEO · 被Meta收购

consumer
Optimizely联合创始人、奥巴马2008竞选数据分析负责人，西罗克创办Rewind AI，2023年以3.5亿美元估值融资1500万美元。2024年4月将公司转型为Limitless，推出99美元的Limitless Pendant——一款常开式AI可穿戴设备，记录并检索对话，24小时内预订超1万台。Limitless累计融资超3300万美元，投资人包括Sam Altman、a16z、First Round与NEA。2025年12月被Meta收购，用于打造个人超级智能可穿戴产品。
Co-founder of Optimizely and former Obama 2008 director of analytics,Siroker founded Rewind AI,which raised $15M at a $350M valuation in 2023. In April 2024 he pivoted the company to Limitless and launched the $99 Limitless Pendant,an always-on AI wearable that records and recalls conversations,drawing 10,000+ pre-orders in 24 hours. Limitless raised $33M+ total from backers including Sam Altman,a16z,First Round and NEA. In December 2025 Meta acquired Limitless to build 'personal superintelligence' wearables.
