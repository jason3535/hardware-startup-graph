Engineer-designer-investor known as the 'father of the iPod' and co-creator of the iPhone at Apple, where he ran the iPod division as SVP. A General Magic alumnus, he founded Nest Labs in 2010 and sold it to Google for $3.2B in 2014. Now invests in deep tech through his firm (Future Shape, operating as Build Collective) and wrote the bestselling builder's manual 'Build' (2022).
