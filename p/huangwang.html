consumerhealthwearable
1975年生。中国科学技术大学应用物理学士（1997）。2013年创办华米科技，与小米合作打造小米手环——全球最畅销健身手环。2018年纽交所IPO（ZEPP）。2021年改名Zepp Health，拓展Amazfit、Zepp Clarity、Zepp Aura品牌。全球出货上亿只穿戴设备。
Born 1975. USTC (University of Science and Technology of China) Applied Physics BS 1997. First founded Huaheng Network Technology 1998. Founded Huami in 2013, partnered with Xiaomi to create the Mi Band — became world's best-selling fitness band. NYSE IPO 2018 (ticker: ZEPP). Rebranded to Zepp Health 2021, expanded into Amazfit, Zepp Clarity, Zepp Aura. Shipped hundreds of millions of wearable devices globally. Direct competitor to Fitbit in global wearables market.
