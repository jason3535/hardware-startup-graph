莱纳·波普Reiner Pope

MatX

MatX 创始人兼 CEO · 专为大模型做的芯片Founder & CEO of MatX · LLM-specific silicon

chips
在谷歌做大模型推理服务，瓶颈对他从来不是抽象概念。2024 年与 Mike Gunter 离职创办 MatX，前提很窄：造一颗只干一件事的芯片——跑和训练大语言模型，通用 GPU 为别的用途花掉的面积统统砍掉。A 轮超 1 亿美元，Spark 领投，Jane Street、Nat Friedman 与 Daniel Gross 跟投；2026 年 2 月完成 5 亿美元 B 轮，由 Leopold Aschenbrenner 的 Situational Awareness 与 Jane Street 共同领投，累计融资超过 6 亿美元。即将推出的 MatX One 号称吞吐高于任何其他芯片，同时保持最低延迟。
Worked on large-model serving at Google, where the bottleneck was never abstract, and left in 2024 with Mike Gunter to found MatX on a narrow premise: build a chip that does nothing but run and train large language models, and give up everything a general-purpose GPU spends area on. Raised a Series A above $100M led by Spark with Jane Street, Nat Friedman and Daniel Gross, then a $500M Series B in February 2026 co-led by Leopold Aschenbrenner's Situational Awareness and Jane Street, taking MatX past $600M raised. The forthcoming MatX One is claimed to beat any other chip on throughput while holding the lowest latency.
