Carnegie Mellon ECE grad. Apple software engineer (2009-2012). Founding Head of Hardware at Oculus VR (2012-2019), worked directly with Palmer Luckey. Left in 2019, founded Framework Computer Jan 2020 — building modular, repairable laptops to fight e-waste. Expanded to Framework Laptop 12, 13, 16, and Desktop by 2025. Offices in SF and Taipei. Leading the right-to-repair hardware movement.
