陶师正 Tao Shizheng

ePropulsion

逸动科技联合创始人 · 电动船外机

consumer
港科大毕业，与大疆创始人汪滔同为李泽湘教授实验室师兄弟。4位创始人均来自港科大。2012年在李泽湘实验室开始研究电动船外机。2014年在松山湖创办逸动科技。自研核心零部件。产品覆盖1-140千瓦电动船外机/船内机+电池。全球电动船外机出货量第二。2022年搬入松山湖1万平新工厂。
HKUST graduate, fellow lab member with DJI founder Frank Wang under Prof. Li Zexiang. All 4 co-founders from HKUST. Started researching electric outboard motors in Li Zexiang's lab 2012. Founded ePropulsion in Songshan Lake 2014 on Li's advice. Self-developed core components after supply chain issues. Products: 1-140kW electric outboard + inboard motors + batteries. Global #2 in electric outboard shipments. HQ moved to new 10K sqm factory in Songshan Lake Robot Base 2022.
