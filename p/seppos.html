芬兰工程师。1977 年发明无线心率监测器 — 全球首款通过胸带向运动员实时传输心率的商用产品。1977 年在芬兰奥卢创办 Polar Electro。Polar 在被 Garmin / Apple 整合前主导可穿戴心率市场 30 年。在教练和运动科学界仍是精度黄金标准。Polar Vantage V3（2024）反映运动+健康混合手表趋势。
Finnish engineer. Invented the wireless heart rate monitor 1977 — first commercial product to deliver real-time HR to athletes via chest strap. Founded Polar Electro 1977 in Oulu, Finland. Polar dominated wearable HR market for 30 years before Garmin / Apple consolidation. Still gold standard for accuracy among coaches and exercise scientists. Polar Vantage V3 (2024) reflects the hybrid sport + health watch trend.
