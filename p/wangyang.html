汪洋 Wang Yang

Aiper

元鼎智能/Aiper创始人 · 泳池机器人线上全球第一

smart-homerobotics
重庆山区出身。前环球资源（国际企业运营）。2015年筹备创业，以家乡圆顶山命名元鼎。2019年发现泳池机器人渗透率仅17%，All in，砍掉所有其他产品线。无线泳池机器人4年做到线上全球第一、总量第二。2025年与全球第一泳池设备商Fluidra战略合作+近10亿融资。进入38+国家。
Born in Chongqing mountain area. Ex-Global Sources (international enterprise operations). Started planning in 2015, named company Yuanding after his hometown's round-top mountain. Initially explored yard automation broadly, then in 2019 discovered pool robot penetration was only 17% — went all-in, cutting all other product lines. Launched Aiper brand wireless pool cleaning robots. Became global #1 in online pool robot sales, #2 overall in 4 years. 2025: completed ~1B yuan strategic round with Fluidra (world's #1 pool equipment company, 50yr history). Entered 38+ countries.
