CEO of Boston Dynamics, the 30+ year robotics pioneer behind Spot, Stretch and Atlas, now owned by Hyundai. At CES (Jan 2026) the company unveiled the production version of the all-electric Atlas humanoid — 56 degrees of freedom, fully rotational joints, 2.3m reach, 50kg lift, autonomous battery swapping — and began production at its Boston HQ. All 2026 Atlas units are committed, shipping to Hyundai's Robotics Metaplant (RMAC) and Google DeepMind, with Hyundai Mobis supplying actuators.
