前苹果与微软项目负责人，塔万加尔于2019年在香港联合创办Brilliant Labs，打造开放、AI优先的智能眼镜。2024年1月推出349美元的多模态AI眼镜Frame，配语音助手。2025年发布Halo，号称全球最薄AI眼镜，支持端侧AI处理、micro-OLED显示与骨传导音频，2026年一季度以349美元发货。Brilliant Labs已从Niantic的John Hanke、Oculus联合创始人Brendan Iribe等人处融资种子轮。
Former Apple and Microsoft program lead,Tavangar co-founded Brilliant Labs in Hong Kong in 2019 to build open,AI-first smart glasses. In January 2024 the company launched Frame,$349 multimodal AI glasses with a voice assistant. In 2025 it unveiled Halo,billed as the 'world's thinnest AI glasses' with on-device AI processing,micro-OLED display and bone-conduction audio,shipping Q1 2026 at $349. Brilliant Labs has raised seed funding from figures including Niantic's John Hanke and Oculus co-founder Brendan Iribe.
