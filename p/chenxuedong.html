陈学栋 Chen Xuedong

Snapmaker

快造/Snapmaker创始人 · Kickstarter 3D打印全球纪录

consumer
1989年生于福建泉州。高中国际机器人奥赛冠军→保送厦大。中航工业西安研究所2年。2016深圳创办快造。三合一：3D打印+激光+CNC。Kickstarter纪录：2017 $227万，2019 Snapmaker 2.0 $785万（当时最高科技众筹），2025 U1 $2061万（3D打印众筹全球历史最高，超1.5亿元）。B轮2025：数亿元，高瓴+美团领投，顺为+同创跟投。
Born 1989 Quanzhou Fujian. Won International Robot Olympiad championship in high school, admitted to Xiamen Univ. Built multiple robots in college. Worked 2 years at AVIC Xian Research Institute. Founded Snapmaker 2016 in Shenzhen Nanshan. 3-in-1: 3D printing + laser engraving + CNC. Kickstarter records: 2017 $2.27M (#3 3D print ever), 2019 Snapmaker 2.0 $7.85M (highest tech crowdfund ever at time), 2025 U1 $20.6M (highest 3D print crowdfund in history, 150M+ yuan). B round 2025: hundreds of millions, led by Hillhouse + Meituan, Shunwei + Cowin followed.
