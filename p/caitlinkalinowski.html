极少数在苹果、Meta、OpenAI 都主管过硬件工程的人。在苹果做 MacBook 结构件，随后在 Meta 十年，从初代 Oculus Rift 一路带到 Quest 与 Orion AR 原型。2024 年 11 月加入 OpenAI 组建机器人与消费硬件团队，2026 年 3 月因公司与美国国防部的协议辞职，公开反对「没有司法监督的监控」和「没有人类授权的致命自主」。她对当下的判断是：AI 硬件才刚开始而不是见顶，因为模型终于撑得起新的形态。
One of the few people who has run hardware engineering at Apple, Meta and OpenAI. Built MacBook enclosures at Apple, then spent a decade at Meta leading VR hardware from the first Oculus Rift through Quest and the Orion AR prototype. Joined OpenAI in November 2024 to stand up its robotics and consumer-hardware effort, and resigned in March 2026 over the company's Department of Defense agreement, objecting publicly to surveillance without judicial oversight and lethal autonomy without human authorization. Her thesis on the current moment is that AI hardware is at the very beginning, not the peak, because the models finally justify new form factors.
