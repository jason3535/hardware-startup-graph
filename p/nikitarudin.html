在苏黎世联邦理工机器人系统实验室读博，做大规模并行强化学习控制足式机器人——正是这条线让「仿真到实机」的行走从稀罕事变成常规操作。他在苏黎世创办 Flexion，补的是多数人形公司缺的那一层：不是身体而是大脑，一套把自然语言任务变成全身运动的控制与规划栈。2025 年 11 月完成 5000 万美元 A 轮，DST Global、英伟达 NVentures、redalpine、Prosus 与 Moonfire 参投；2026 年 6 月发布面向长时程任务的 Reflect v1.0。
Did his PhD at ETH Zurich's Robotic Systems Lab on massively parallel reinforcement learning for legged robots — the line of work that made sim-to-real locomotion routine rather than remarkable. Co-founded Flexion in Zurich to build the layer most humanoid companies are missing: not the body but the brain, a whole-body control and planning stack that takes a natural-language task and turns it into motion. Raised a $50M Series A in November 2025 from DST Global, NVIDIA's NVentures, redalpine, Prosus and Moonfire, and shipped Reflect v1.0 in June 2026 for long-horizon missions.
